Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Fyrion community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Communicating professionally and constructively.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberate intimidation.
- Threats of violence.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexualized behavior or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption of community discussions.
- Other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

They may take appropriate and proportionate action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

Possible actions may include:

- A warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions from project spaces.
- Permanent removal from project spaces.
- Other reasonable actions necessary to protect the community.

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the Fyrion project maintainers.

When making a report, provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

- What happened.
- When and where it happened.
- Who was involved.
- Any relevant messages, screenshots, or other evidence.

Please avoid publicly sharing sensitive information about the people involved.

Security vulnerabilities should not be reported through the Code of Conduct process. Follow ""SECURITY.md"" (SECURITY.md) for security-related reports.

Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will review reports fairly and, where appropriate, confidentially.

Reports should be handled without retaliation against individuals who make good-faith reports.

Maintainers may remove or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project community.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within official Fyrion project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or community.

It may also apply to behavior outside official project spaces when that behavior has a direct and significant impact on the safety or functioning of the Fyrion community.
